The Business Verification agent (`businessVerification`) checks whether each self-employed borrower and the loan's vesting entity are registered and active with their state's Secretary of State, using the Cobalt Intelligence provider.

<Note>
This agent is **entitlement-gated**: it runs only for lenders holding an explicit `enabled=true` entitlement row. The router's zero-rows fallback (all active agents for an un-seeded lender) does not grant it. See `requiresExplicitGrant` in [Agent catalog and pipeline](/agents/catalog-and-dependencies).
</Note>

## What it checks

The agent finds each subject in the Secretary of State database and runs three checks:

| Check key | What it answers |
|---|---|
| `found` | Was a state record matched with at least medium confidence? |
| `active` | Does the record show the entity is currently active? |
| `formation` | Was the entity formed before or by the declared start date? |

`formation` is `not_applicable` for vesting entities — there is no declared start date to compare.

Check results follow a closed vocabulary: `passed`, `failed`, `warning`, `missing`, `incomplete`, or `not_applicable`.

### Match confidence

After a record is found, the agent scores the match:

| Confidence | Condition | `found` check |
|---|---|---|
| `high` | Both name and address agree | `passed` |
| `medium` | Either name or address agrees | `passed` (with a `Warning` on the `found` check, signalling the partial match) |
| `low` | Neither agrees | treated as not found; all checks report `missing` |

Name agreement uses normalized name matching (NAME-0). Address agreement checks the street number and ZIP5 of the declared business address against the state record.

A medium match (name only or address only) surfaces as a `Warning` with a next step. It keeps its `active` and `formation` results but counts toward needs-review rather than as a clean pass.

### Delaware special case

Delaware publishes no normalized entity status. For a Delaware record without a `normalizedStatus`, the agent reads the franchise-tax line as a proxy:

- `"Franchise tax is in good standing"` (trimmed, case-insensitive) → treated as `active`
- Any other value, or none → treated as `unknown`, which yields `incomplete`, never `failed`

Records stored before the franchise-tax line was captured also read as `unknown`.

## Subjects

The agent identifies subjects from the URLA extraction result and the loan's MISMO vesting entity.

**Self-employed borrowers.** For each borrower, the agent reads only the *current* self-employment (`section1BorrowerInfo.currentEmployment`). Previous and additional jobs are deliberately not checked. A borrower with no `isOwnBusinessOrSelfEmployed` flag, or with a blank employer name, contributes no subject.

**Vesting entity.** The MISMO vesting entity produces one subject unless its cleaned name matches a self-employed borrower's business — in which case it is covered by that borrower's lookup and not checked separately.

### Deduplication

Two self-employed subjects are the same business when their cleaned names and search states match, and their street number and ZIP agree wherever both borrowers declared them. Co-borrowers naming the same business produce one lookup, not two. The kept subject carries the most complete address across all its members.

A subject is **unusable** (and all its checks report `missing`) when:
- the business name normalizes to fewer than 3 characters, or
- the business address carries no resolvable two-letter state code.

## How a lookup works

For each usable subject the agent acquires or reuses a `business_verification_lookups` row.

### Lookup key and 120-day reuse

The lookup key is `kind|name|state|street_number|zip5` (cleaned). Vesting entities append the property state. A lookup completed within the past **120 days** (Fannie Mae standard) is reused; an older lookup is retired (`retired_at`) and a new one is created.

<Note>
Self-employed lookups now carry `SELF_EMPLOYED_PLANNER_VERSION = "2"` in their key (LOA-3355). A lookup matched by the old rules (planner version 1) is not reused; the next audit searches again. On re-audit, the first run after deploy always makes a fresh paid search, plus up to 2 extra ID lookups per self-employed business.
</Note>

The partial unique index `bv_lookups_loan_key_live_unique` ensures at most one live (pending or complete, un-retired, not served from a saved copy) lookup per loan and key. When two audits for the same loan need the same lookup simultaneously, one picks up the other's result.

### Search strategy

The search planner decides provider calls per subject type:

- **Self-employed:** name-and-address identification (`search-planner.ts::identifyByNameAndAddress`, introduced in LOA-3355 with `SELF_EMPLOYED_PLANNER_VERSION = "2"`). Every company whose cleaned name equals the 1003's is checked against the 1003 address (physical or mailing). That includes same-name alternatives from the provider. Different-name records at the 1003 address and near-name alternatives whose own listing shows the 1003 street and ZIP are also weighed. At most `MAX_SAME_NAME_LOOKUPS` (2) extra ID lookups are made per business, stopping as soon as a company is confirmed at the address.

  Self-employed outcomes:

  | Outcome | When | `found` result |
  |---|---|---|
  | One company at the address | High confidence match | `passed` |
  | One same-name company only (nothing at the address) | Medium confidence (name only) | `passed` with `Warning` |
  | Several candidates, or any unresolved alternative | `ambiguous` — no company is chosen | `missing` (all three checks missing; counts toward needs-review, never as `failed`) |
  | All candidates looked up and found empty | Not found | `missing` |
  | A same-name lookup fails | Lookup fails; next audit retries | failure |
  | Provider saves a copy withholding alternatives | Treated as `ambiguous`; page explains why | `missing` |

  When the result is `ambiguous`, `checks.ts::deriveBusiness` renders: "The business could not be identified: N {State} records could be {name}." followed by each candidate with its status, city, and whether it is at the 1003 address. `active` and `formation` read "The business could not be identified, so the rule could not be verified."

- **Vesting entity:** Cobalt typeahead search (stateless), then an ID lookup on the typeahead result. If typeahead fails, falls back to a name search in the property state.

### Concurrency and lease

The worker uses a lease (`lease_token` + `lease_expires_at`) on each lookup to serialize rounds across parallel workers. A lease expires if the holder stalls; a stranded lookup can be claimed by another run. The `paid_call_sent_at` column marks the first paid provider call; the `next_poll_at` column schedules the worker's next round.

A lookup that receives no result within 5 minutes of its first provider call is sealed `timed_out` (`status: failed`, `failure_reason: timed_out`).

## Triggering

| Channel | How triggered |
|---|---|
| EPC | Pre-enrolled in the agent ledger before phase 0; dispatched after phase 0 completes (when URLA extraction is available). |
| Partner API | Runs by default when `agents[]` is empty and the lender is entitled. Holds the audit open until lookups finish, the same as `appraisalEvaluation`. Pass a non-empty `agents[]` to run only those agents instead. |

<Note>
Business Verification results are **not exposed on the Partner API results surface** — only `summary.agentStatuses` includes its status. Lender-facing access to findings is through the audit viewer.
</Note>

## Data model

Migration `0166_milky_snowbird` adds two tables. Both FK to `audit_runs` with `ON DELETE CASCADE`.

### business_verification_lookups

One row per Secretary of State lookup. A completed lookup is reused by later audits of the same loan within 120 days.

| Column | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `id` | uuid PK | `gen_random_uuid()` |
| `lender_id` | uuid NOT NULL | FK → `lenders` |
| `loan_id` | uuid | FK → `loans`; nullable when the audit has no loan |
| `origin_audit_run_id` | uuid NOT NULL | FK → `audit_runs` (cascade) — the audit that created the lookup |
| `subject_kind` | text NOT NULL | `self_employed` or `vesting_entity` |
| `lookup_key` | text NOT NULL | Canonical `kind\|name\|state\|street_number\|zip5` key |
| `query_name` | text NOT NULL | The name string sent to the provider |
| `query_state` | text | Two-letter state code sent to the provider |
| `status` | text NOT NULL | `pending`, `complete`, or `failed` |
| `failure_reason` | text | Set on `failed` rows |
| `outcome` | text | `matched`, `not_found`, or `low_confidence` |
| `search_path` | jsonb | Sequence of provider calls planned and executed |
| `raw_responses` | jsonb | Provider response payloads |
| `record` | jsonb | The matched `StateRecord` (null when outcome ≠ `matched`) |
|| `candidates` | jsonb | Near-match candidates when outcome ≠ `matched`. Each candidate entry now includes optional `status` (normalised entity status, from the state listing), `city`, and `atDeclaredAddress` (boolean) fields added in LOA-3355. `status` falls back to the franchise-tax line for Delaware. |
| `served_from_saved_copy` | boolean NOT NULL | True when the result came from a prior lookup's file cache |
| `effective_date` | date | Date the record was effective |
| `screenshot` | jsonb | Captured screenshot reference |
| `filings` | jsonb | Captured filing documents |
| `lease_token` | uuid | Concurrency fence: the current holder's token |
| `lease_expires_at` | timestamptz | When the current lease expires |
| `paid_call_sent_at` | timestamptz | Time of the first paid provider call (starts the 5-minute timeout) |
| `retired_at` | timestamptz | Set when the lookup is superseded by a newer one for the same key |
| `completed_at` | timestamptz | When the lookup reached terminal status |

Unique partial index `bv_lookups_loan_key_live_unique` on `(loan_id, lookup_key)` where `status IN ('pending', 'complete') AND NOT served_from_saved_copy AND retired_at IS NULL`.

### business_verification_subjects

One row per subject an audit checks. Position is stable (`bv_subjects_audit_position_unique` on `(audit_run_id, position)`).

| Column | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `id` | uuid PK | `gen_random_uuid()` |
| `audit_run_id` | uuid NOT NULL | FK → `audit_runs` (cascade) |
| `lookup_id` | uuid | FK → `business_verification_lookups`; null for unusable subjects |
| `subject_kind` | text NOT NULL | `self_employed` or `vesting_entity` |
| `borrower_label` | text | Which borrower (null for the vesting entity) |
| `declared_name` | text NOT NULL | Name from the 1003 / MISMO |
| `declared_address` | jsonb | Street, city, state, postal code |
| `declared_start_date` | text | `YYYY-MM` or `YYYY-MM-DD` from the 1003 |
| `search_state` | text | Two-letter state searched first |
| `property_state` | text | Subject property state (vesting entity's fallback search state) |
| `unusable_reason` | text | `name_too_short` or `state_unresolved` |
| `reused` | boolean NOT NULL | True when this subject was answered from a prior lookup |
| `position` | integer NOT NULL | Stable ordinal within the audit run |

## Portal API

Two endpoints under `PortalAuditsBusinessVerificationController` (`api/v1/portal/audits/:auditId/business-verification`), scoped to the caller's lender via `PortalAuditsAuthGuard`:

| Method | Path | Returns |
|---|---|---|
| GET | `/api/v1/portal/audits/:auditId/business-verification` | `BusinessVerificationResponse` |
| GET | `/api/v1/portal/audits/:auditId/business-verification/screenshots` | Screenshot images (base64) for all matched lookups |

`BusinessVerificationResponse` shape:

| Field | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `auditRunId` | string | |
| `status` | string | `complete`, `not_applicable`, `failed`, `pending`, `skipped`, or `absent` |
| `businesses` | `BusinessVerificationBusiness[]` | One entry per subject |
| `counts` | `CheckCounts` | Aggregate `ran`, `passed`, `failed`, `couldntCheck`, `warnings`, `notApplicable`, `needsReview` |

`not_applicable` is returned when the loan has no self-employment or vesting entity to check (`no_current_self_employment_or_vesting_entity`). `absent` is returned when the agent was not included in the audit's plan.

Portal routes: `/loan-audits/[auditRunId]/business-verification` (staff viewer) and the next.js App Router BFF at `api/v2/audits/[auditRunId]/business-verification`.

### State filings (LOA-3354)

When the Cobalt Intelligence lookup returns filing documents for a business, the portal shows them as clickable links — each opens the state's own filing page in a new tab. The `BusinessVerificationFilings` component (`packages/shared-ui/src/audit/business-verification/BusinessVerificationFilings.tsx`) renders nothing when the state returned no filings.

**Key filing badges** appear above the full list. The `keyFilings` helper selects up to three:

| Badge | Which filing |
|---|---|
| Formed | The oldest dated filing; falls back to the last filing when none is dated |
| Latest | The most recent filing that is not the formation or a status-change filing |
| Status change | The most recent filing whose name matches a dissolution, revocation, or reinstatement keyword |

The Status change badge is rendered in a red/danger style. If Latest or Status change coincides with the Formed filing, that badge is omitted.

**All filings panel.** A "View all N filings" button opens a slide-over panel that groups filings by type. The `groupFilings` helper strips year numbers from names to form group labels (`"Annual Report 2025"` and `"Annual Report - 2024"` become one `"Annual Report"` group). Each group header shows the filing count, year range, and whether the group contains the formation filing. Within each group, 3 filings are shown initially with a "Show N more" toggle.
